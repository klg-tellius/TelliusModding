# Staves

*Every staff in Path of Radiance, with its range, uses and price*

Staves heal, cure or inflict a status instead of attacking; what each one does is in [Items & Weapons](./items-weapons.md) and, for the status staves, [Combat Mechanics](./combat-mechanics.md). Weapon EXP is the staff rank experience a use gives.

The table lists every weapon of this type in the retail North American data (`FE8Data.bin`), in table order. Rank is the weapon rank needed to wield it ("—": no rank, a personal or special weapon); Price is the shop price in gold (the item record stores a price per use; blank: not sold). How might, hit and critical enter a battle is in [Combat Mechanics](./combat-mechanics.md), and the item record itself in [Items & Weapons](./items-weapons.md). <span class="confidence confidence--confirmed">data</span>
