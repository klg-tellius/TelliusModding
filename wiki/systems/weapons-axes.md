# Axes

*Every axe in Path of Radiance, with its numbers and special properties*

Swords, lances and axes form the weapon triangle: a sword has the advantage over an axe, an axe over a lance and a lance over a sword. The side with the advantage gets +10 Hit and +1 Attack, the other side −10 and −1; see [Combat Mechanics](./combat-mechanics.md#the-weapon-triangle). <span class="confidence confidence--confirmed">engine</span>

Axes are the heavy, hard-hitting side of the triangle. Every axe also carries the "door" category, which marks it as a door-breaking weapon. Hand axes and Tomahawks reach 1–2 tiles.

The table lists every weapon of this type in the retail North American data (`FE8Data.bin`), in table order. Rank is the weapon rank needed to wield it ("—": no rank, a personal or special weapon); Price is the shop price in gold (the item record stores a price per use; blank: not sold). How might, hit and critical enter a battle is in [Combat Mechanics](./combat-mechanics.md), and the item record itself in [Items & Weapons](./items-weapons.md). <span class="confidence confidence--confirmed">data</span>
