# Sources and attribution

Tellius Archive documents formats and behavior for interoperability. It does not distribute game discs, executables, extracted artwork, fonts, music, or dialogue archives.

## Evidence classes

`tellius-archive-research`
: Findings reproduced from the project's local format readers, aggregate surveys, executable analysis, and in-game tests. Public articles summarize the result; raw, game-derived dumps remain outside the published site.

`telliusmodding-source`
: Behavior implemented and tested in the public TelliusModding source tree.

`project-credits`
: Third-party tools and research leads listed in the repository's `CREDITS.md`. Their inclusion does not transfer ownership or imply endorsement.

## Licensing boundary

Original wiki prose and original diagrams are available under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). Git history records authorship.

Fire Emblem, Path of Radiance, Radiant Dawn, and game content belong to Nintendo and Intelligent Systems. Screenshots, names, trademarks, quoted game text, and other third-party material are not relicensed under CC BY-SA. Each imported third-party asset must carry its own attribution and license note.

## Citing a claim

Prefer reproducible evidence: name the file or system, the game and region, the comparison or test, and any important limitations. Do not turn an inference into a fact by removing its confidence label.
